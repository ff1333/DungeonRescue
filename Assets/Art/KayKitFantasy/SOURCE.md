Integration:

- `PlayerRogue.fbx` becomes the player adventurer.
- `PrincessMage.fbx` becomes the rescued princess.
- `SkeletonMinion.fbx` and `SkeletonWarrior.fbx` become normal and elite monsters.
- `SkeletonMage.fbx` becomes the independent Abyss Warden boss.
- Selected dungeon props provide pillars, torches, banners, crates, rubble, treasure and cage details.
- `FogboundFantasyArtBuilder` selects and renames animation clips, assigns URP materials, attaches equipment and writes runtime prefabs under `Assets/Resources`.

The project does not claim the third-party meshes, textures or authored animation clips as original modeling or animation work.
