# Third-Party Art

## KayKit fantasy packs

The v1.5.1 fantasy art pass uses selected assets from KayKit Adventurers, KayKit Skeletons and KayKit Dungeon Remastered by Kay Lousberg. All three packs are licensed under CC0 1.0 Universal and permit modification and redistribution, including commercial use.

- Adventurers: https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0
- Skeletons: https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Skeletons-1.0
- Dungeon Remastered: https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Dungeon-Remastered-1.0
- Included licenses and integration record: `Assets/Art/KayKitFantasy/`

KayKit supplies the player, princess, monster, boss, equipment and dungeon-prop meshes, textures and authored animation clips. Gameplay code, procedural layout, combat, AI, camera, UI and runtime assembly remain project code.

## Quaternius Zombie Apocalypse Kit

The legacy v1.4 character sources and the retained sniper-rifle mesh are by Quaternius, licensed under CC0 1.0 Universal. The v1.5.1 runtime character prefabs use KayKit instead.

- Official pack: https://quaternius.com/packs/zombieapocalypsekit.html
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Included license and provenance: Assets/Art/QuaterniusZombieApocalypse/License.txt and SOURCE.md.

CC0 permits use, modification and redistribution, including commercial use. The local project records the source even though attribution is not required by CC0. No purchase, account registration or separately installed modeling tool is needed to run this integrated version.

## Noto Sans CJK SC

The unmodified Noto Sans CJK SC Regular font is bundled for Chinese/English UI. It is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

- Upstream: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
- Font: `Assets/Resources/Localization/NotoSansCJKsc-Regular.otf`
- Full license: `Assets/Resources/Localization/OFL.txt`

The project-generated ambient loop and procedural combat sounds are not part of the Quaternius pack.
